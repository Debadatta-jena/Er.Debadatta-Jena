Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Er. Debadattaa Jena (Founder & CEO of PRYONX and CLYVEXA), together with its websites and services, collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal data. It is drafted in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India. By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide voluntarily

When you contact us through WhatsApp, email, or any enquiry channel, you may choose to share:

  • Your name and designation
  • Your phone number and/or email address
  • The content of your message, project details, or business information you send us

b) Information collected automatically

When you visit this website, certain technical information may be logged automatically by our hosting and third-party service providers, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, referring URLs, and approximate location derived from IP

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or any mechanism that sells your data.

c) On-site FAQ assistant

The optional assistant on this site is a pre-scripted FAQ widget that runs entirely inside your browser. It uses only fixed questions and answers written in the page's code — it stores nothing, records nothing, and sends nothing to any server. Closing the chat or refreshing the page clears the conversation completely.

2. Purpose & Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data only for legitimate, specified purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and communicate about potential projects or services
  • To deliver, support, and improve the services offered by PRYONX and CLYVEXA
  • To maintain website security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot
  • To comply with legal obligations

The legal basis for processing is your consent — provided voluntarily when you choose to contact us — and legitimate purposes as permitted under the DPDP Act, 2023.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website is a static portfolio and does not set first-party tracking cookies. However, it loads resources from the following trusted third parties to render correctly. These providers may process limited technical data (such as your IP address):

  • Google Fonts — for typography
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) — for Font Awesome icons
  • Vercel — website hosting and standard server logs
  • WhatsApp / Meta — when you click a WhatsApp link, you interact under Meta's own privacy terms

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with service providers strictly necessary to operate (e.g., hosting), or when required by law, regulation, or a legitimate legal request.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above — for example, to continue a business conversation you started — or as required to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Thereafter it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Our third-party providers (such as Google, Cloudflare, and Vercel) may store or process data on servers located outside India. By using this website, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encrypted HTTPS delivery and access limitation — to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a data principal, you have the right to:

  • Access & Information — obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been processed
  • Correction & Erasure — request correction, completion, updating, or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw your consent at any time, which may prevent us from providing certain services
  • Grievance Redressal — lodge a grievance, which we will address within the timelines prescribed by law
  • Nominate — nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

9. Children's Data

This website is not directed at children under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
